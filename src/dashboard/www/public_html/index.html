About

The Ukraine NowPop dashboard is a visualisation tool to explore population estimates for 1,138 hromadas in Ukraine by age and sex groups from February 2023 to Novembre 2025. Those estimates were produced by the NowPop team at the University of Oxford, in collaboration with WHO Ukraine.

These estimates are derived from aggregated and anonymised mobile phone data from Vodafone combined with baseline hromada-level population totals from the Statistics Service of Ukraine, monthly border crossing , age-sex demographics of Ukrainian with temporarary protected status in neighboring countries and age-sex demographics of Ukrainian returnees. The Vodafone data represent counts of Vodafone subscribers in Ukraine whose home locations were in each hromada each month, and counts of subscribers who had moved between hromadas each month.

The NowPop team developed a bespoke rapid-response methodology to estimate population sizes and flows among hromadas each month based on estimating and updating Vodafone penetration rate.

No population estimates were included for Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetska and Luhanksa as well as an additional 103 hromadas because Vodafone mobile phone data were not available from these areas of Ukraine consistently through time

Missing subscriber counts were imputed for hromadas (n=3) with three or fewer consecutive timesteps of missing data by using the last observed timestep. Hromadas that had more than three consecutive missing timesteps were removed from the scope.

The “home” location of Vodafone subscribers is defined based on their most frequent nighttime location over the three-month period prior to our population estimate reference date. This does not capture sudden short-term changes of location.

Population estimates at oblast level are available to anyone. For population estimates at the raion or oblast level, please contact us.

All source code for these project components are available from the GitHub repository: https://github.com/OxfordDemSci/UkraineNowPop.

Population distribution

The 'Population distribution' panel shows the distribution of the population of the selected geographical unit by age and sex from the date selected. Please hover on the graph to see the number of people. The graph reacts to the filter on Geography, Sex, Ages and Time.

Mobility

The 'Mobility to/from'tab shows a chord plot of the most important mobility corridors. It gets updated when selecting a geographical unit, an age group or a sex to display the most common mobility corridors to and from that spatial unit. The hover windows shows the values. Proportions are in % and computed from the destination perspective that is the proportion of the total number of people of the selected age and sex that move to the selected destination. It is possible to expand the window by clicking on the top-right corner.

Info

The 'Info' tab shows the general information on population sizes about the selected geographical unit, age group and sex.
